About Mahiyanganaya

A sacred town with a wider cultural landscape

Mahiyanganaya sits in Sri Lanka's Uva Province near the Mahaweli River and is strongly associated with Buddhist pilgrimage, ancient irrigation heritage and the living culture of nearby Dambana.

The site voice should stay practical and respectful: encourage modest dress at temples, local guides for community visits, and care around reservoir edges, wildlife and village life.
