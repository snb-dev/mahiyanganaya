Nagadeepa Viharaya

A quieter heritage site in the Badulla District, useful for visitors who want to go beyond the main temple circuit.

Overview

Visit Uva identifies this Nagadeepa Viharaya as an ancient temple complex in Uraniya village in the Badulla District, distinct from the better-known Nagadeepa in the north of Sri Lanka.
