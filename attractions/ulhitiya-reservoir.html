Ulhitiya Reservoir

A tranquil reservoir near Mahiyanganaya with water, hills, birdlife and sunset views.

Overview

Visit Uva describes Ulhitiya Reservoir as a scenic man-made reservoir near Mahiyanganaya, serving irrigation and local water needs while offering calm landscapes for visitors.

Things to Do

Plan for photography, birdwatching, short nature walks, sunset views and quiet reservoir-side stops. Any boating or fishing guidance should be verified locally before publishing.

Travel Tips

Confirm access roads, safe viewpoints and weather before visiting. Reservoir edges can be slippery, and water levels may change after rain.

Quick Facts

  • Best forScenery, birdwatching, sunset
  • Suggested time45 minutes to 2 hours
  • Pair withMapakada Lake or Sorabora Wewa
