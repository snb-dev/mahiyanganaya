Besama Natural Pool

A peaceful stream-fed rock pool near Mahiyanganaya for cooling off, relaxing by the water and adding a nature stop to a day trip.

Overview

Besama Natural Pool is described by Uva tourism references as one of Mahiyanganaya's peaceful scenic nature attractions, surrounded by greenery and fed by cool, clean streams. It is best treated as a low-key local escape rather than a heavily serviced tourist site.

The name “Besama” is commonly associated with a basin-like pool shape, making the spot appealing for visitors looking for a simple swim, a quiet picnic edge or a short nature break.

What to Do

Plan for a relaxed visit: take photos, enjoy the stream setting, cool off in suitable conditions and keep the visit short enough to combine with Sorabora Wewa, Dambana or the town circuit.

Facilities appear limited from public listings, so visitors should bring drinking water, towels, a change of clothes and any snacks they need, then carry all waste back out.

Access and Safety

Public listings place Besama Natural Pool around the Mahiyanganaya/Welanpela area, with Trip.com listing the map reference as 93CC+XXG, Mahiyanganaya. Confirm the route locally before publishing final directions or sending visitors there.

Visit during daylight, avoid entering the water after heavy rain, check depth before swimming, wear footwear with grip around rocks and supervise children closely.
